I am an architectural designer with over 2 years of experience in architectural design and an additional 2 years in urban planning. My work spans residential, commercial, and institutional projects — from schematic design and design development through to construction drawings and BIM documentation.

I bring a multidisciplinary approach to every project, combining spatial sensitivity with technical precision. My background in urban planning allows me to understand the broader context in which buildings exist, while my hands-on experience with BIM tools ensures that design intent is communicated with clarity and accuracy throughout the process.

Whether working on a small-scale interior transformation or a large institutional brief, I am committed to thoughtful design that responds to people, place, and purpose.
