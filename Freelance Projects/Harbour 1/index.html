Beypore Harbour

Type

Architectural Walkthrough / Video Visualization

Role

3D Modelling, Animation, Rendering

Year

2021

Location

Beypore, Kerala, India

Project Summary

An architectural walkthrough video visualizing a proposed harbour development at Beypore, Kerala. The animation communicates the spatial scale, material quality, and experiential sequence of the development, giving the client a clear sense of the proposal before construction.

The video captures movement through key spaces — waterfront promenades, built edges, and public areas — emphasising the relationship between architecture and the harbour setting.
