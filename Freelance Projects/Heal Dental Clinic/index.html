Heal Dental Clinic

Type

Adaptive Reuse / Interior Design / Healthcare

Role

Schematic Design, Design Development, Construction Drawings, BIM / Revit, AI-Assisted Renderings

Year

2022

Location

Kerala, India

Project Summary

Adaptive reuse of a residential structure into a welcoming dental clinic, balancing clinical function with a calm, patient-centred environment. The project involved reorganising the spatial layout to accommodate patient flow, clinical adjacencies, and infection control requirements.

The design development included schematic layouts, construction drawings, Revit modelling, and AI-assisted interior renderings to communicate the finished environment to the client.
