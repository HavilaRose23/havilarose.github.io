Project Summary

The project consists of a residence that has to be renovated with modern interiors. A series of interior visualizations exploring contemporary residential spaces with a focus on warm material palettes, ambient lighting, and functional spatial planning. The design emphasises simplicity, comfort, and subtle accent elements to enhance visual depth.

Design Approach

Bedrooms

Bedrooms are designed with soft neutral tones with accent feature walls. The focus was to create a calm, restful atmosphere — each bedroom has a controlled accent colour that gives it a distinct identity while maintaining the overall palette coherence.

Living Room

The living room features a modern TV unit with integrated storage. A symmetrical composition is used for the TV unit design, grounding the space and providing visual balance against the warm material backdrop.

Colour Palette

A warm neutral base complemented by natural wood tones and muted accent colours. Dark elements are introduced selectively to create contrast, while warm lighting enhances spatial comfort.
