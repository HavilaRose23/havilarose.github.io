Project Summary

The apartment was designed for 8 rental housing units arranged across 3 floors on a plot of 0.089 Acre (362.269 Sq M). All 8 units are 2BHK but across 3 distinct typologies, responding to the constraints of the site while maximising usable area.

The ground floor consists of two housing units and car parking for three four-wheelers. The first and second floors each accommodate 3 housing units. This is a residential apartment on a small plot, designed as affordable housing.
