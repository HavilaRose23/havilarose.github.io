Project Summary

A commercial facade study for an urban retail complex, exploring material language, facade rhythm, and urban street presence. The design responds to the surrounding context through a considered composition of solids, voids, and surface articulation.

The project involved facade development, technical elevation drawings, and 3D visualizations to communicate the design intent to the client and design team.
