Project Summary

Structural shop drawings produced for a headquarters building, documenting footing arrangements, foundation sections, and ground floor slab and beam layouts. The drawings are developed in Autodesk Revit and provide construction-ready details for the structural team.

The documentation covers the complete foundation system — from the general arrangement layout through to individual footing sections — alongside coordinated slab and beam plans and sections for the ground floor.
